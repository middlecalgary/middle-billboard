Inglewood Night Market · Inglewood, SE Calgary

The Inglewood Night Market is Calgary's most beloved outdoor summer market. Held on select evenings in the heart of Inglewood, the market brings together 100+ local vendors selling handmade goods, vintage treasures, artisan crafts, and unique finds. Add live music, food trucks, and the magic of Inglewood at night for one of Calgary's truly unmissable summer experiences.
